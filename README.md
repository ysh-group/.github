# Better Health. Better Research. Longer, More Capable Lives.

We are building a global collaboration ecosystem for better human health,
capability, and longevity.

Our work brings together people, scientists, clinicians, healthcare professionals, engineers, institutions, and AI systems around a shared goal: turning useful questions and ideas into trusted knowledge, responsible research, and real impact.

## What We Explore

- Evidence-based health, fitness, nutrition, skincare, and healthy routines
- Human performance, brain capability, preventive health, and healthy aging
- Aging biology, medicine, biotechnology, bioengineering, and regenerative medicine
- Genome engineering, scientific knowledge, research collaboration, and AI

## Our Direction

The consumer and scientific sides of health should strengthen each other. People need practical ways to learn, improve, and connect, while researchers need better ways to organize knowledge, discover collaborators, analyze evidence, and pursue new ideas.

We are building infrastructure that connects these communities and supports the journey from knowledge and data to hypotheses, research, evidence, and better tools for discovery.

## Mission

To help people live healthier, stronger, more capable, and better-informed lives while accelerating responsible progress in health, science, medicine, biotechnology, and AI.

Our projects support learning, positive habits, scientific collaboration, knowledge discovery, and the development of AI systems that can help people understand health information and help researchers work more effectively.

## Contribution & Sponsorship

We welcome contributors, collaborators, and sponsors.

- Code, docs, and experiments: open issues and pull requests in the relevant repositories. Look for issues labeled `good-first-issue` to get started.
- Research & data partnerships: contact project maintainers to discuss data governance, agreements, and collaboration frameworks.
- Sponsorship & funding: support feature development, infrastructure, or fellowship programs by opening a sponsorship issue or using a repository's FUNDING file.

If you want to help immediately:
1. Browse our repositories and open issues.
2. Submit documentation, small fixes, or tests via pull requests.
3. Join conversations in issues or Discussions for each project.

## Governance & Values

We prioritize ethics, transparency, reproducibility, and privacy. Our projects aim to follow clear contribution guidelines, maintain Architecture Decision Records (ADRs), and operate under a Code of Conduct.

## Security & Compliance

Handling health and research data responsibly is a core requirement. We design systems with privacy and compliance in mind (e.g., data minimization, consent management, pseudonymization, and appropriate access controls). Projects that process sensitive data should include SECURITY and compliance documents (HIPAA, GDPR considerations) and follow established reporting and mitigation procedures.
