# YSH IT Nexus — a community for better health and better research

We are a community building practical healthcare platforms and research tools that help people live healthier, longer, and more capable lives. Our purpose is to bring together people, scientists, clinicians, and technologists to create open, privacy-first infrastructure for health and research.

## Organization purpose

- Build open, privacy-first software and research infrastructure for consumer health and scientific work.
- Bridge the gap between everyday health tools and researcher-grade systems so both communities benefit.
- Encourage transparent, reproducible, and ethical approaches to health, biotechnology, and AI.

## Quick Start — Automatic User Registration

We've streamlined the process of joining our community. **You can register automatically by opening a single GitHub issue** with your information.

### How to Register

1. **Go to** [Issues in this repository](https://github.com/ysh-group/.github/issues)
2. **Click** "New issue"
3. **Use this template** for the issue title and description:

**Title format:**
```
[REGISTER] Your Name
```

**Description (include all fields):**
```
Email: your.email@example.com
Purpose: Briefly describe why you want to join YSH IT Nexus (e.g., "Contributing to healthcare platform", "Participating in research", "Learning about privacy-first systems")
```

### Example
```
Title: [REGISTER] Jane Smith

Description:
Email: jane.smith@example.com
Purpose: I'm a healthcare researcher interested in contributing to privacy-preserving data workflows and collaborating on FHIR integration.
```

### What happens next?
- Our automation system will detect your registration issue within minutes
- You'll receive an automated confirmation comment on your issue
- We'll add you to the organization and send welcome information to your email
- You'll gain access to our repositories and can start contributing immediately

**Note:** Make sure your GitHub email is public or use the email field in the registration issue to ensure we can contact you.

## Contribution — everyone is welcome

This organization is community-first. If you want to help, you can contribute in many ways:
- Code and docs: open issues and submit pull requests. Look for issues labeled `good-first-issue`.
- Research & data: propose data partnerships, share reproducible analyses, or discuss study design.
- Design & product: help with UX, accessibility, and user flows for privacy-preserving health apps.
- Sponsorship & funding: support infrastructure, fellowships, or feature development.
- Community & governance: help write contribution guidelines, ADRs, and moderation policies.

See CONTRIBUTING.md and CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md in the org for details. If those files are not present yet, open an issue and we'll add templates and guidance.

## Current project

Our current focus is designing and building an extensible healthcare platform and research ecosystem that includes:
- A privacy-first user experience for people to learn, track, and improve health habits.
- Research tools and data workflows that let scientists discover collaborators, manage experiments, and analyze evidence.
- AI-assisted tooling to surface insights while preserving privacy (pseudonymization, consent controls, and governance).
- Interoperability with standards like FHIR where appropriate, strong security controls, and compliance-minded design.
