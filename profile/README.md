# YSH Group — a community for better health and better research

We are a community building practical healthcare platforms and research tools that help people live healthier, longer, and more capable lives. Our purpose is to bring together people, scientists, clinicians, engineers, institutions, and AI systems so useful questions and ideas can be turned into trusted knowledge, responsible research, and real-world impact.

## Organization purpose

- Build open, privacy-first software and research infrastructure for consumer health and scientific work.
- Bridge the gap between everyday health tools and researcher-grade systems so both communities benefit.
- Encourage transparent, reproducible, and ethical approaches to health, biotechnology, and AI.

## Contribution — everyone is welcome

This organization is community-first. If you want to help, you can contribute in many ways:
- Code and docs: open issues and submit pull requests. Look for issues labeled `good-first-issue`.
- Research & data: propose data partnerships, share reproducible analyses, or discuss study design.
- Design & product: help with UX, accessibility, and user flows for privacy-preserving health apps.
- Sponsorship & funding: support infrastructure, fellowships, or feature development.
- Community & governance: help write contribution guidelines, ADRs, and moderation policies.

See CONTRIBUTING.md and CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md in the org for details. If those files are not present yet, open an issue and we’ll add templates and guidance.

## Current project

Our current focus is designing and building an extensible healthcare platform and research ecosystem that includes:
- A privacy-first user experience for people to learn, track, and improve health habits.
- Research tools and data workflows that let scientists discover collaborators, manage experiments, and analyze evidence.
- AI-assisted tooling to surface insights while preserving privacy (pseudonymization, consent controls, and governance).
- Interoperability with standards like FHIR where appropriate, strong security controls, and compliance-minded design.

If you want to get involved now: browse the org repositories, open issues, or start a discussion outlining how you’d like to contribute (code, research, funding, or other support). We’ll be glad to collaborate.
